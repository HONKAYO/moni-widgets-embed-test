How one trade moves cash, debt and PAPER

The trader always faces Papertrade. A win can become an immediate payout or queue debt. A loss adds cash to the system and mints PAPER.

If the trader losescash inflow
1
The loss stays in the protocol

It builds the cash pot and can clear older winning claims.

2
PAPER is minted to the loser

Early losses earn more PAPER. The rate falls as cumulative LP gains rise.

3
The trader can stake PAPER

Stakers receive a share of fees and cash above the protocol's target balance.

The shared engine

Cash pot + payout queue

Losses refill the same pool that pays winners.

  • First: pay the oldest queued profit.
  • Then: rebuild a positive cash balance.
  • Above target: excess cash can go to PAPER stakers.
feeds

PAPER ownership loop

Losing traders receive a claim on future platform income.

  • Early farmers: mint more PAPER per dollar lost.
  • Later farmers: add cash but receive fewer tokens.
  • Value depends on: fees, trading losses and retention.
!
The loop breaks when the queue grows faster than new losses.

Winners wait longer → traders leave → fewer losses arrive → the queue clears even more slowly.
