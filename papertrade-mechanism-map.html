Papertrade · mechanism map

The full loop: cash, queue and PAPER

Papertrade takes the other side of every position. Winners pull cash out; losers refill the system and receive PAPER.

01 · OPEN

Trader deposits USDC

Papertrade records a leveraged BTC or ETH position. Hyperliquid supplies the midpoint price.

02 · SETTLE

Papertrade calculates the result

The payout curve adjusts winning P&L, then the protocol applies its fee.

Trader winsCash pot pays now; any shortfall enters the FIFO queue.
Trader losesUSDC refills the pot and PAPER is minted to the trader.
03 · RECYCLE

Losses fund the whole loop

New losses pay old claims, rebuild the cash balance and support PAPER staking rewards.

The mechanism

Winners consume liquidity; losers create it. PAPER gives the losing side a claim on future fees and excess cash.

The failure loop

Queue grows → traders leave → fewer losses arrive → the queue clears more slowly.
