Robinhood Chain · two-pool TVL model

How a rounding error becomes a rival

Robinhood Chain launched at ~$15M against Base's ~$4.3B. It won't close that gap by "converting 10% of Robinhood users" — no chain grows that way. It fills from two real pools: existing onchain capital that rotates in, and net-new retail the brokerage funnel brings that wasn't onchain before.

Where the TVL comes from

Two non-overlapping pools. Pool 1 is measured in dollars of existing capital (so a Robinhood user who's already onchain isn't double-counted); Pool 2 is strictly net-new users the funnel pulls onchain for the first time.
