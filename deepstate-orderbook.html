Deepstate · what it is

It's an orderbook. Onchain.

Buyers post the price they'll pay. Sellers post the price they'll take. When the two meet, Deepstate pairs them and settles the trade — no liquidity pool in the middle.

That's the whole idea. A book of real buy and sell orders, matched onchain. The clever engineering makes it cheap enough to run on Ethereum. The open question isn't the matching — it's whether enough buyers and sellers show up to fill the book.
