The retro validation ladder

Not every action should earn the same claim on the future network.

A useful retro gets progressively harder: from showing up, to doing work that proves a future compute market can exist.

1Now

DID onboarding

An agent establishes a repeatable identity and can return.

2

Testnet action

It runs a real client flow, not a social quest.

3

Independent supply

An unaffiliated miner or validator becomes reachable.

4

Verified paid job

Work completes, verifies and settles without team trust.

5

Repeat demand

The buyer returns after the incentive has disappeared.

→The step that matters most is #4. Before an outside buyer pays an outside provider for a verified job, FLOP has onboarding evidence, not market evidence.
