DEEP per dollar of posted capital collapses across the first thirty days.
Both curves are constants, not market conditions. The emission side decays logarithmically from the moment the first order rests; the size requirement climbs geometrically to 1,000,000 USDG and 5,000 NVDA over thirty days. Nothing that happens on the book moves either one. Whoever is standing there earliest is paid the most for the least.

Curves as written in the contract: C(t) = 500M · ln(1+t/30) / ln(1+395/30) per side. “Per $1” divides the day’s emission by the minimum qualifying order at that day.
