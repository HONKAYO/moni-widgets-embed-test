NFTX v4 · how it works

One NFT's journey

The whole v4 mechanism, one step at a time.Say the collection floor is 1 ETH and your NFT is worth 1.5.
Step 1

Deposit any NFT, name your price

Old NFTX only worked for floor pieces. In v4 you drop any NFT into the collection pool and set your own price, here 1.5 ETH. You don't have to wait for a buyer to get moving.Illustrative example to explain the mechanism · floor = 1 ETH

Step 2

Take the floor as cash, right now

You instantly get 1 floor-token (= 1 ETH), fully liquid, sell it this second. The extra 0.5 ETH stays as your listing and pays out only when a buyer takes it.

1.0 ETH now
0.5 later

Step 3

The pool keeps prices honest

Price it too high and a listing fee quietly bleeds until it sells, so fantasy prices cost you. Spot someone else's NFT listed too cheap? You can pay a fee to re-list it higher and keep the difference, without ever buying it. Collection experts fix the prices and get paid for it.

Step 4

Buyer trades up, LPs get paid

A buyer swaps floor-tokens for your exact NFT, and you collect the 0.5 ETH premium. Every list, re-list and swap sends fees to the liquidity providers keeping the market open. That floor-token is now the price of the whole collection, not just its cheapest corner.
