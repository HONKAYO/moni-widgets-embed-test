Ascend · planned system map

The launchpad stays in the market

HYPE yield buys exposure to new launches. Trading fees then flow back into creators, ecosystem assets and the infrastructure around those markets.

01 · STAKE

HYPE stays as the base position

Ascend routes deposits into kHYPE. Its normal staking yield buys qualifying ecosystem tokens and distributes them to stakers.

02 · LAUNCH + TRADE

Volume powers the system

Projects launch in closed pools. Every token trade pays a 1% fee before the separate swap fee and network cost.

03 · SPLIT

Creators get paid; the rest stays at work

The recipient gets 25%, or 50% when approved. Remaining protocol revenue buys HYPE and KNTQ equally.
